My Favourite Recipes

Here are some simple and delicious recipes.

Pasta

★★★★★

Creamy white sauce pasta made with vegetables, milk and cheese.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup pasta
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1 tablespoon flour
  • Mixed vegetables
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Cheese
  • Chilli flakes

Process

  1. Boil the pasta and drain the water.
  2. Heat butter in a pan and add flour.
  3. Add milk slowly while stirring.
  4. Add vegetables, salt and pepper.
  5. Add the boiled pasta and mix well.
  6. Add cheese and chilli flakes.
  7. Cook for a few minutes and serve hot.

Rating: 5/5

Vada Pav

★★★★☆

A popular Mumbai street food made with spicy potato vada and pav.

Ingredients

  • 2 pav
  • 2 potatoes
  • Besan
  • Green chilli
  • Ginger
  • Turmeric powder
  • Salt
  • Oil
  • Green chutney

Process

  1. Boil and mash the potatoes.
  2. Add chilli, ginger, turmeric and salt.
  3. Make small potato balls.
  4. Prepare a besan batter.
  5. Dip the potato balls in the batter.
  6. Deep fry until golden brown.
  7. Place the vada inside the pav with chutney.

Rating: 4/5

Hyderabadi Veg Biryani

★★★★★

Flavourful basmati rice cooked with vegetables and aromatic spices.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • Mixed vegetables
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tomato
  • Curd
  • Ginger-garlic paste
  • Biryani masala
  • Mint and coriander leaves
  • Salt and oil

Process

  1. Wash and soak the basmati rice.
  2. Cook the rice until partly done.
  3. Cook vegetables with spices.
  4. Add curd and biryani masala.
  5. Layer the rice over the vegetables.
  6. Add mint and coriander leaves.
  7. Cover and cook on low heat.

Rating: 5/5

Pizza

★★★★☆

Cheesy homemade pizza topped with vegetables and delicious pizza sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 pizza base
  • Pizza sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Onion
  • Capsicum
  • Tomato
  • Sweet corn
  • Oregano
  • Chilli flakes

Process

  1. Place the pizza base on a tray.
  2. Spread pizza sauce over the base.
  3. Add vegetables and cheese.
  4. Sprinkle oregano and chilli flakes.
  5. Bake until the cheese melts.
  6. Cut into slices and serve hot.

Rating: 4/5

Burger

★★★★☆

A simple homemade burger with a vegetable patty and fresh vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 2 burger buns
  • Vegetable patties
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato
  • Onion
  • Cheese slice
  • Mayonnaise
  • Tomato ketchup

Process

  1. Toast the burger buns lightly.
  2. Cook the vegetable patties.
  3. Spread mayonnaise and ketchup.
  4. Add lettuce, tomato and onion.
  5. Place the patty and cheese.
  6. Cover with the top bun and serve.

Rating: 4/5

Noodles

★★★★☆

Tasty vegetable noodles made with noodles, fresh vegetables and simple spices.

Ingredients

  • 1 packet noodles
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 capsicum
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon chilli sauce
  • Salt
  • Oil

Process

  1. Boil the noodles and drain the water.
  2. Heat oil in a pan.
  3. Add garlic and onion and cook for a minute.
  4. Add carrot and capsicum.
  5. Add soy sauce and chilli sauce.
  6. Add the boiled noodles and mix well.
  7. Cook for a few minutes and serve hot.

Rating: 4/5
